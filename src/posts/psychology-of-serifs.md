## Expectations from print

Readers bring expectations from print. Serifs can signal authority and rhythm on long reads, while sans families often read as interface-native.

## Matching voice to medium

The honest move is to match the voice of the publication to the medium you are actually shipping, then test with real paragraphs at real sizes.
