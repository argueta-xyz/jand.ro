Structured thought wants a structure you can trust. Markdown stays close to the source: headings, lists, links, and code without a proprietary canvas in between.

## Plain text as structure

When you archive ideas as plain text, diffing, searching, and migrating stay honest. The page becomes a durable object rather than a fleeting layout state.

## Archival honesty

Formats that age well share a bias toward transparency: what you store is what you mean, without hidden layers that only one vendor can interpret.
