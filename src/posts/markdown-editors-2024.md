## What matters first

A good editor gets out of the way. Fast open, predictable shortcuts, and exports that do not mangle your markup.

## Markdown as source of truth

This survey focuses on tools that treat Markdown as the source of truth, not as an afterthought behind a WYSIWYG layer.
