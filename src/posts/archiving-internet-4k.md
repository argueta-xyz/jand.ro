## Fidelity and cost

Preservation is not only crawl frequency. It is also fidelity: color, texture, and motion that future readers can study without guessing what was lost in compression.

## What higher resolution buys

Higher resolution raises storage and integrity costs, but it also raises the ceiling for scholarship and restoration.
